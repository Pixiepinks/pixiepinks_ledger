Metric definitions & historical coverage

Unique contacts are distinct normalized numbers sending an inbound message. First recorded contacts use all retained inbound history. Returning contacts had an earlier recorded message. Invalid/unresolved numbers remain in message counts but cannot contribute to unique-number counts.

Each provider message counts once. Captions stay with their media message; each separately sent collection product is one message. Staff, AI, system and unknown triggers are separate. Plain inquiries, purchases and interests do not establish marketing consent.

Some older accepted send claims have no chat history. Their durable attempt timestamp is used and reported separately; exact acceptance time is unavailable. Historical sender IDs or categories may be unknown. Unattributed historical sends remain under All staff and are not reassigned to current owners.

Country is the phone numbering region, not physical location. Operational queues are live and pipeline stages are current, not historical snapshots. No financial value is inferred from product-interest text.
